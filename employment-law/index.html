§ IV.iOverview

Employment law.

We have represented both employers and employees at mediation to resolve personal grievances and other employment disputes.

The firm has provided advice to employers on redundancy and restructuring.

We can also represent clients in the Employment Relations Authority and provide advice on a range of employment issues, including employment agreements and restraint of trade.

If you're facing a workplace dispute, going through a disciplinary process, dealing with restructuring issues, or have concerns about your employment agreement, we can offer tailored advice. Reach out to our team today to chat about your employment matter.
