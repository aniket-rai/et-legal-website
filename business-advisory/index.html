§ V.iOverview

Business advisory.

Sameer Rai, Associate, had a successful career as a business owner in India and New Zealand before becoming a lawyer, and holds degrees in economics and business.

Sameer has advised businesses on setup, contract review, risk and compliance, and business development.

We can provide advice on business structuring and setup, contracts, risk and compliance, dispute resolution, and business development. We understand the commercial, strategic and time-sensitive nature of business decisions.

If you're starting a business, sorting out contracts, managing risks, or facing tricky disputes, we're here to help with practical legal advice that supports your business goals.

§ V.iiStructuring

Business structuring and setup.

Choosing the right business structure is key when starting out or planning to grow. We assist business owners in setting up legal frameworks that support growth, operations, and risk management.

We offer advice on:

  1. Company formation and governance structures
  2. Shareholder arrangements and ownership structures
  3. Partnership and joint venture agreements
  4. Business acquisition and startup structuring
  5. Directors' duties and governance matters
  6. Commercial risk management

Every business operates in its own distinct way, so there's no one-size-fits-all structure. We begin by understanding your commercial goals, then recommend practical legal structures that fit your day-to-day operations, finances, and long-term objectives.

§ V.iiiContracts

Contracts and commercial agreements.

Well-drafted contracts and commercial agreements are essential for protecting your business, minimising risks, and fostering strong working relationships. We assist businesses in drafting, reviewing, and negotiating agreements.

A solid commercial agreement should be more than just legally sound; it should also be practical and effective for everyday operations. We aim to provide clear, strategic advice that safeguards you while supporting your business growth.

If disputes or uncertainties come up, we help you understand your contractual position and find effective solutions.

§ V.ivCompliance

Risk and compliance advice.

Running a business today means dealing with a complex network of laws and regulations. We provide clear risk and compliance guidance to help you identify potential issues early and manage legal risks confidently.

Seeking advice before problems occur helps you avoid disputes, keep your operations running smoothly, and build a stronger business for the long term.
