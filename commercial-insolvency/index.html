§ III.iOverview

Commercial and insolvency.

The firm has acted for commercial landlords and tenants in disputes over repair obligations and unlawful eviction. We have also worked for construction contractors, restaurant proprietors, and supermarket owners in building disputes, director and shareholder disagreements, and franchise issues.

Mike has a longstanding interest in insolvency law and has worked with debtor companies and directors to restructure obligations and enable continued trading.

We have successfully acted for creditors in debt recovery proceedings and in applications for company liquidation. We can guide creditors through the winding-up process after the liquidator is appointed, and help with all company insolvency and recovery matters.

If you're facing a commercial dispute, a contract disagreement, unpaid debts, or corporate insolvency, we're here to help. Get in touch with us today.
